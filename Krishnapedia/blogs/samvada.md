**Samvada**: Logical argument based on facts. Both arguers remain humble that they know only partial truth and argue with an intent to arrive at truth.

**Jalpa**: When two people argue egoistically. Both believe that they have the truth and just fight with each other.

**Vitanda**: The sole goal is finding faults, picking holes, and tearing down the other person's reasoning.

Only engage in Samvada - or redirect other arguments back to samvada.
