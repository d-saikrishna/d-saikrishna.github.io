# Infrastructure built atop caste system.

In “Discovery of India”, Jawaharlal Nehru described India this way:

> She was like some ancient palimpsest on which layer upon layer of thought and reverie had been inscribed , and yet no succeeding layer had completely hidden or  erased what had been written previously.

I came across an interesting observation that stands testimony to what Nehru wrote.

In Telangana and Rayalseema, rain-fed tanks are crucial for irrigation and drinking water supply. The tank-bunds are also our public leisure spaces. These tanks are built by Kakatiya, Vijayanagara kings and so there is some cultural pride associated with them as well.

These were well thought engineering designs. Constructured in serial, when one tank overflows, another tank gets filled and so there’s no flood. Drought and flash floods we see today in these regions can be linked to the poor maintenance of these tanks by current administration. _How wise our ancestors were?_

But who maintained these tanks previously? In the essay, “A Year of Drought”, K Balagopal writes that the maintenance (which was manual labour like building bunds, clearing mud from tank beds, etc) was done by Dalits through an exploitative system (Vetti in Telangana). Dalits work the tanks without getting paid, and they don’t even get to use it. The tanks however function, watering the fields and preventing floods.

Now, with dilution of caste, at whatever rate, maintenance of tanks can’t be relegated to such feudal ways of past. Governments’ Public Works Departments (PWD) and Panchayats are now responsible to maintain tanks. Both complain that they don’t get enough funds for tank maintenance (There was no habit of paying for this work to begin with). And so we live with drought and flood.

Also, its not so easy as in the past to stop Dalits from using tanks today. And not everyone is happy about it.

Its not tanks alone. In India, a lot of civic infrastructure (sanitation especially) is built and maintained atop caste system. We have to learn other ways of doing so.
